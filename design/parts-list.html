Before you order

  • Prices are approximate as of Oct 2, 2026. Check each listing before buying.
  • Start with Phase 1. The tile code reads one data URL, so moving to your own radios later is a config change, not a rewrite.
  • Outdoor ship antenna upgrade for later: a marine VHF antenna tuned for 162 MHz (Shakespeare 5101 is a common pick). Height and a clear view of the water matter more than the antenna itself.
  • Feed your plane data to FlightAware once Phase 2 is running and they give you a free premium account.
  • To-do mode needs no parts. Tasks live in a free Cloudflare Worker that Claude can add to and check off.
  • No soldering anywhere. Everything plugs together or screws in.
